About

I am a Staff Research Scientist at Ant Healthcare, developing frontier AI systems for healthcare. Previously, I was a postdoctoral scholar at Stanford University with Prof. Ruijiang Li, where I worked on AI foundation models for biomedicine, with a focus on computational pathology, radiology, and spatial biology. I received my PhD from HKU MMLab in 2024, advised by Prof. Ping Luo. My research spans generative models, vision-language models, and AI agents, translating frontier AI into clinically impactful tools for diagnosis, treatment, and biomedical discovery.
